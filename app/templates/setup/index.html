HTTPS / Security

ConfigLake stores secrets — they must be protected in transit.

Self-signed certificate Easiest setup
ConfigLake generates a certificate automatically and saves it to certs/. Your browser will show a one-time warning — click "Advanced → Proceed".

Certificate details (O and OU are optional)

My own certificate
You have a certificate from Let's Encrypt, ZeroSSL, or another CA.
Behind a reverse proxy
Nginx, Caddy, or another proxy terminates HTTPS upstream. ConfigLake runs on plain HTTP internally. See deploy/ for ready-to-use configs.
HTTP only Not safe for production
Secrets will travel unencrypted. Local development only.
